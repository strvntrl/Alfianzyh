My story

From a first line of HTML to professional industry

Chapter 01

My story

I first discovered my interest in programming back in junior high school. The very first programming language I learned was HTML. It fascinated me how lines of code could shape what we see on a screen. However, I had to put that interest on hold during high school due to a tight schedule and not having a proper device to practice coding on my own.

As I approached graduation, I decided to start learning again from scratch. Even though I still didn’t have a laptop at that time, I was eager to learn so I began watching tutorials on YouTube and taking notes to prepare myself.

Chapter 02

Growing with Code and Digital Skills

Back in high school, I also enjoyed editing short videos, usually nature-themed. Sometimes I was given the responsibility to edit video assignments, which continued into my college years. These experiences taught me how important visuals and storytelling are in delivering a message.

In college, my interest in web development grew stronger. Beyond that, I became drawn to data and Information Systems Management, as I believe that great technology should be supported by strong, structured management and by decisions grounded in data.

Chapter 03

Where I am now

Today I have hands-on experience as a Full-Stack Web2/Web3 and System Developer for professional industry. What started as watching tutorials and taking notes has turned into shipping software that real teams and real production floors rely on.

As a Front End Web Developer Intern at Minilemon Technology, I translated UI/UX designs into responsive interfaces, integrated APIs, and adapted the application through a backend migration from Convex to Prisma ORM. As a System Developer Intern at PT. Exedy Prima Indonesia, I built a Clutch Disc Production Monitoring Dashboard, a Production Monitoring & Control (PMC) System, and a Production Report System integrated with Microsoft SQL Server.

Alongside that, I build Web3 products — DeFi interfaces on Robinhood Chain and transaction tooling for autonomous agents on Solana — and turn raw data into dashboards that support decision-making. I’m now focusing on Information Systems Management and Digital Humanism: building systems that address organizational needs while keeping the people who use them at the center.
